Case study: Solé Coffee

The challenge
A new roastery needed to stand out in a crowded shelf and explain why its beans cost more, in under a minute.
What we made
A 60-second brand film, six motion-graphic packaging reveals and a month of vertical social cuts, all built from one visual idea: the first light of the day.
What happened
The film became the centrepiece of the launch campaign and drove most of the first month's online orders.
